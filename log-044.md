# Log 044 --- Completing Book Zero

## Completing Book Zero

The reconstruction of the Beeson--Narboux--Wiedijk Book Zero over the
CGJteamLab Hilbert foundation has now been completed.

The original purpose of this work was not only to reconstruct the BNW corpus,
but also to observe how much elementary synthetic geometry can be derived from
the Hilbert axioms before introducing additional interface assumptions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Book Zero

According to Beeson, Narboux, and Wiedijk, Book Zero is not a replacement for
Euclid's Book I.

It contains elementary geometric results that Euclid uses implicitly before
the formal development of Book I.

The present reconstruction therefore completes only this preliminary layer of
the BNW development.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The interface

During the first stages of CGJteamLab several temporary interface axioms were
introduced whenever a classical proof required elementary geometry that had
not yet been reconstructed.

Book Zero has now allowed almost all of these assumptions to be replaced by
ordinary derived theorems.

Results concerning rays, same-side arguments, segment comparison, congruence
transformations and angle addition are now part of the derived theory instead
of the primitive interface.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Remaining assumptions

Only two primitive assumptions remain.

```lean
bookZero_nullSegment1
bookZero_nullSegment2
```

These assumptions are not missing Book Zero theorems.

Instead, they describe the treatment of null segments adopted by the present
formalization.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Current picture

```text
Hilbert axioms
      |
      v
Book Zero
      |
      v
Derived interface
      |
      v
Classical proofs
```

At the moment this picture should be regarded as a working hypothesis rather
than a final architecture.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Next step

The completion of Book Zero finishes only the preliminary part of the BNW
development.

The next stage is the reconstruction of Euclid's Book I.

During that work we will continue to observe which derived results become
stable components of the reusable geometry interface and which remain local to
individual proofs.
