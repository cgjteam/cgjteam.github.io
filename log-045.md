# Log 045 --- Beginning the Reconstruction of Book I

## From Book Zero to Book I

The reconstruction of Book Zero established a working layer of elementary synthetic geometry above the Hilbert axioms.

The next stage of the experiment is to reconstruct Book I using this layer.

The purpose is not simply to formalize another collection of propositions. The reconstruction provides a direct test of whether the elementary results developed in Book Zero actually form a useful interface for larger classical proofs.

## First objective

The immediate objective is deliberately limited:

- reconstruct the first part of Book I,
- reuse Book Zero results whenever possible,
- avoid introducing new axioms merely to complete individual proofs,
- record which additional geometric constructions or lemmas are genuinely required.

This changes the role of Book Zero. It is no longer being developed in isolation. It is now being tested by the proofs for which it was intended.

## Current direction

The reconstruction will proceed proposition by proposition.

Rather than designing a complete Book I interface in advance, the required structure will be allowed to emerge from the formal proofs themselves.

This continues the dependency-driven method used throughout the CGJteam Lab geometry project.
