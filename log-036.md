\# Log 036 — Refactoring the Tarski Route



\## Objective



This session focused on simplifying the architecture of the Tarski formalization after the successful reconstruction of the complete proof route.



The objective was not to change the mathematics of the development, but to remove temporary architectural layers that were no longer necessary and expose the true logical structure of the Tarski route.



The resulting proof architecture now mirrors the organization previously established for the Hilbert and Suppes developments.



\## Removing temporary interfaces



Two temporary interface classes were eliminated.



The first was `TarskiMidpointExistence`, which had originally isolated the construction of midpoint existence from the rest of the development.



Its functionality was replaced by a direct theorem depending only on the neutral Tarski framework together with the current deferred construction assumptions.



The second removed component was `TarskiMedianIntersection`.



Instead of assuming the intersection of two medians as an independent interface theorem, the result is now derived directly from the neutral Tarski theory using Inner Pasch.



Consequently, the high-level proof no longer depends on either auxiliary interface.



\## Refactoring the Finlay proof



The internal organization of the Finlay proof was simplified.



The auxiliary theorem previously responsible for constructing the symmetric point was removed.



The public theorem now performs the required construction internally before invoking the proof core.



The proof hierarchy therefore became



```

FinlayTarskiStep1

FinlayTarskiStep2

FinlayTarskiStep3

FinlayTarskiStep4

&#x20;       │

&#x20;       ▼

FinlayTarskiCore

&#x20;       │

&#x20;       ▼

FinlayTarski

```



This organization closely follows the structure already adopted for the Hilbert development.



\## Public theorem



The public theorem now states only the mathematical result.



For every non-collinear triangle, there exist



\- the midpoint of AC,

\- the midpoint of AB,

\- the intersection of the two medians,

\- the midpoint of BC,



such that the intersection point also lies on the third median.



No auxiliary interface assumptions appear in the theorem statement.



\## Remaining deferred dependency



The architectural audit also clarified the only remaining deferred component.



Although `FinlayTarski` now depends only on the neutral Tarski framework at the interface level, the current implementation of midpoint existence still relies on temporary placeholder assumptions.



These placeholders isolate a single missing construction rather than affecting the overall proof architecture.



This makes the remaining research objective precise:



replace the temporary midpoint construction by a genuine derivation from the Tarski theory.



\## Result



The Tarski route now has a clean layered architecture comparable to the Hilbert and Suppes developments.



Temporary interface classes have been eliminated, the public theorem exposes only the intended mathematical statement, and the remaining deferred dependency has been isolated to a single construction problem.



The architectural reconstruction of the Tarski route can therefore be considered complete, while the future work is now clearly focused on replacing the remaining placeholder construction with a formal proof.
